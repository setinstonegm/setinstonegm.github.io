StoneSync Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how StoneSync handles information when connecting QuickBooks Online, Microsoft 365, Excel, and related services.

1. Information We Access

StoneSync may access business data authorized by the user or administrator, including customer, estimate, quote, contact, and workbook data from connected services.

2. How We Use Information

We use this information to sync authorized business records between connected systems, generate workflow records, improve operational visibility, and support business reporting.

3. Sharing of Information

StoneSync does not sell personal information. Data is only shared with service providers or systems necessary to operate the application, such as QuickBooks Online and Microsoft 365, as authorized by the business.

4. Data Retention

Information may be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for operational, legal, security, and business recordkeeping purposes.

5. Security

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards are used to protect authorized business data. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Third-Party Services

StoneSync connects to third-party platforms. Their separate terms and privacy policies may also apply.

7. Your Choices

If you are an authorized business user and have questions about access, correction, deletion, or connected account permissions, contact your business administrator.
